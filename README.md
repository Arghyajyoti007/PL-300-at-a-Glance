## Module 1: Get Started with Microsoft Data Analytics

### Chapter 1: Discover Data Analysis

#### Introduction

Every business has its own dataset. However, this data can be useless if it isn't represented in the form of a story to the correct stakeholders. The story itself guides the appropriate stakeholders in making informed business decisions, which eventually helps the business grow.

As a Data Analyst, our main goal is to collaborate with Data Engineers and Data Scientists to analyze data, build a meaningful story from it, and present it to the right stakeholders to help the organization make effective business decisions.

#### Overview of Data Analysis

Data analysis is the process of identifying, cleaning, transforming, and modeling data to discover meaningful and useful information.

To analyze data, the core components of analytics are divided into the following categories:

* **Descriptive Analysis:** It answers the question ***"What has happened?"*** This type of analysis summarizes historical data and provides business stakeholders with information in the form of KPIs (Key Performance Indicators).

* **Diagnostic Analysis:** This helps answer the question ***"Why did the event occur?"*** It analyzes performance indicators and provides insights by following these steps:

  * Find anomalies in the data.
  * Collect additional data related to the anomalies.
  * Use statistical techniques to identify relationships between the anomalies and their underlying causes.

* **Predictive Analysis:** This type of analysis attempts to predict and identify future trends and the likelihood of an event recurring. It answers the question ***"What will happen?"*** based on historical data.

* **Prescriptive Analysis:** This helps answer the question ***"What actions should be taken to achieve the goal or target?"*** It supports business decision-making and often relies on Machine Learning (ML) to identify patterns in large semantic models.

* **Artificial Intelligence (AI):** AI helps us ***answer questions using existing datasets***. It enables systems to process vast amounts of data, recognize patterns, and deliver insights with minimal human intervention. AI is being used across a wide range of applications.

#### Roles in Data

* **Business Analyst:** A Business Analyst is closer to the business and is a specialist in interpreting data and translating insights from visualizations into business requirements and decisions.

* **Data Analyst:** A Data Analyst's main responsibilities include profiling, cleaning, and transforming data; designing and building scalable and effective semantic models; and turning data into meaningful visualizations and reports using tools such as Microsoft Power BI. Data Analysts can also enable and implement advanced analytics capabilities within reports. They may also manage and implement security procedures for Power BI assets, including reports, dashboards, workspaces, and underlying semantic models.

* **Data Engineer:** The primary responsibilities of Data Engineers include using on-premises and cloud data services and tools to extract, transform, and load data from multiple sources. Data Engineers collaborate with business stakeholders to identify and meet data requirements.

* **Analytics Engineer:** Analytics Engineers bridge the gap between data engineering and analysis by curating data assets in data lakes or lakehouses, ensuring data quality, and enabling self-service analytics. They create semantic models in Power BI to prepare and serve data effectively and may also prepare other data stores, such as data warehouses or lakehouses.

* **Data Scientist:** Data Scientists perform advanced analytics to extract value from data. Their work can range from descriptive analytics to predictive analytics. Descriptive analytics evaluates data through a process known as Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA). Predictive analytics uses Machine Learning to apply modeling techniques that can detect anomalies or patterns.

#### Tasks of a Data Analyst

<img width="1038" height="481" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9e5364ed-ee29-48d2-9811-8af1fa545e7a" />

* **Prepare:** Data preparation is the process of taking raw data and turning it into information that is trusted and understandable. It involves ensuring data integrity, correcting incorrect or inaccurate data, identifying missing data, converting data from one structure or type to another, and making data more readable. It also involves understanding how data will be obtained and connected, along with the performance implications of those decisions. Privacy and security assurances are also important.

* **Model:** Data modeling is the process of determining how tables are related to each other. This process is performed by defining and creating relationships between tables. An effective semantic model makes reports more accurate, allows data to be explored more efficiently, decreases report development time, and simplifies future report maintenance.

* **Visualize:** A well-designed report should tell a compelling story about the data, enabling business decision-makers to quickly gain the insights they need. By using appropriate visualizations and interactions, you can create an effective report that guides readers through the content quickly and efficiently, allowing them to follow a narrative through the data. Reports should be designed with accessibility in mind from the outset so that no special modifications are required in the future.

* **Analyze:** In your role as a Data Analyst, you should understand the analytical capabilities of Power BI and use those capabilities to find insights, identify patterns and trends, predict outcomes, and communicate those insights in a way that everyone can understand.

* **Manage:** As a Data Analyst, you are responsible for managing Power BI assets, overseeing the sharing and distribution of items such as reports and dashboards, and ensuring the security of Power BI assets. Apps can be a valuable distribution method for your content and allow easier management for large audiences.

  Sharing and discovering content is important for ensuring that the right people get the answers they need. It is also important to ensure that content remains secure. Power BI helps reduce data silos through the use of shared semantic models and allows you to reuse data that you have prepared and modeled. For key business data, endorsing a semantic model as **Certified** can help ensure trust in that data.

### Chapter 2: Get Started Building with Power BI

#### Introduction
Microsoft Power BI is a complete reporting solution that offers data preparation, data visualization, distribution, and management through development tools and an online platform.

#### Use Power BI
There are three primary components to Power BI:

* Power BI Desktop (desktop application) : Power BI Desktop is the development tool available to data analysts and other report creators.
* Power BI service (online platform) : Power BI service allows you to organize, manage, and distribute your reports and other Power BI items. 
* Power BI Mobile (cross-platform mobile app) : Power BI Mobile allows consumers to view reports in a mobile-optimized format.

***Explore the flow of Power BI***
The flow of Power BI is:

1. Connect to data with Power BI Desktop.
1. Transform data with Power Query Editor (comes with Power BI Desktop).
1. Model data with Power BI Desktop.
1. Create visualizations and reports with Power BI Desktop.
1. Publish report to Power BI service.
1. Distribute and manage reports in the Power BI service.
