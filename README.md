## Module 1 : Discover Data Analysis 
### Introduction
Every Business has it's own dataset. But these data will be useless if the data would not represented as a form of story to correct stakeholders. Because the story itself guide the correct stakeholders to make appropriate business decisions, that will eventually help the business to grow. As a Data Analyst out main goal is to engage with Data Engineers, Data Scientists, to analyze the data and make a story out of it, and to represent it to correct stakeholders to help organization make business decisions. 

### Overview of data analysis
Data analysis is the process of identifying, cleaning, transforming, and modeling data to discover meaningful and useful information. 
To analyze data, core components of analytics are divided into the following categories:

- **Descriptive Analysis:** It answers the question ***What has happened***. This analysis summarize the data and provide business stakeholders if the in form of KPI (Key Performance Indicators)
- **Diagnostic Analysis:** This helps us to answer the question ***Why the event occurs***. It analyze the performance indicators and provide us insights by following below steps:
  - Try to find anomalies in data.
  - Collect more data in the anomalies
  - Use statistical techniques to find relationship between them
- **Predictive Analysis:** 
- Prescriptive
- Artificial Intelligence (AI)
