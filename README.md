# PL 300 at a Glance

## Module 1 : Discover Data Analysis
### Introduction
Every Business has it's own dataset. But these data will be useless if the data would not represented as a form of story to correct stakeholders. Because the story itself guide the correct stakeholders to make appropriate business decisions, that will eventually help the business to grow. As a Data Analyst out main goal is to engage with Data Engineers, Data Scientists, to analyze the data and make a story out of it, and to represent it to correct stakeholders to help organization make business decisions.
